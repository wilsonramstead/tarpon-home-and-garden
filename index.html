The shop

The kind of place
you can get lost in.

A whimsical corner store filled with pots, wall decor, yard art and so much more — a Tarpon Springs favorite that shoppers say is an experience all its own.

Wander in and you'll find shelves and walls loaded with hand-painted Talavera pottery, vivid metal yard art, coastal and beach pieces, hand-carved wood, whimsical garden critters and gifts for indoors and out. The pieces are unique, the prices are reasonable, and there's far more in the back than you'd ever guess from the sidewalk.

Best of all is the welcome — the folks who own and work here are known for being helpful and kind, right down to carrying your purchases out to the car. It's a store people happily drive across Tampa Bay for.

What you'll find

A little bit of
everything wonderful.

From a statement pot for the patio to a tiny gift for a friend, the shelves are stocked with color, character and Florida charm.

Talavera & Ceramic Pottery

A huge selection of hand-painted Talavera plant pots, planters and ceramic pieces in every color and pattern — perfect for tropical plants and patio gardens.

Metal Yard & Wall Art

Bright metal artwork to hang or plant — pelicans, geckos, dragonflies, fish, starfish and flowers that bring a wall, fence or flower bed to life.

Coastal & Beach Decor

Pelicans and pink flamingos, driftwood, seahorses, beachy signs and pool-area accents — the Gulf-coast character that makes a Florida home feel like home.

Hand-Carved Wood

Carved wooden giraffes, owls, pelicans, angels and more — natural, handcrafted statement pieces for a shelf, an entryway or a garden corner.

Whimsical Garden Critters

Playful, colorful characters — quirky metal birds, ladybugs, frogs and more — the fun, unexpected finds that make people smile and shop again.

Indoor Gifts & Signs

Wall signs, mirrors, indoor accents and gift-sized finds — colorful, affordable pieces that are just as at home inside as they are out in the garden.
